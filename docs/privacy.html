Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Applies to Windows, Linux, and Android apps

Legacy Device & Router Opener runs 100% locally on your device. It collects, stores, logs, or transmits zero personal data, analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or router credentials to any external server.

1. Local loopback operation

The app starts a local proxy strictly on loopback (localhost / 127.0.0.1 on Windows and Linux; 127.0.0.1 on Android) so your modern browser or built-in viewer can open older routers, printers, switches, and server management consoles on your local network.

2. Data collection & network traffic

  • Zero personal information, usage analytics, advertising IDs, crash logs, or telemetry are collected or sent anywhere.
  • All proxied network traffic stays strictly between your device and the local IP address or hostname you choose to open.
  • Router usernames, passwords, and session cookies are never logged or transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • The app makes zero background external network requests. External links (Website or GitHub) only open in your system browser when you explicitly tap them.

3. On-device storage

  • Android: Your selected device address, hardware profile, and UI language are stored solely on your device in private app preferences (SharedPreferences with cloud backup disabled). Session cookies for your router are held in memory while the proxy is active and cleared when you reset login or stop the proxy.
  • Windows & Linux: Active router session cookies and connection logs exist only in memory while the app is open and are discarded on exit. Your browser stores only your UI language choice in local browser storage on localhost.

4. Device permissions (Android)

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — detect your local Wi-Fi gateway IP and connect to your router on the local network.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS — keep the local proxy running when switching to Chrome or another browser and show an active stop control.

5. Open source & contact

The full source code is publicly auditable under the GNU GPLv3 license at github.com/Alaa7Hany/legacy-router-opener.
